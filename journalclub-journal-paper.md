# Journal Club Slides — Journal Paper

For **Nature / Science / Cell and major sub-journals**.

## Goal
Explain the **scientific question, conceptual advance, key evidence, mechanism, and significance**.

## Recommended Slides

1. **Paper Overview**
   - Title, authors, affiliations, journal, year
   - One representative figure

2. **Why This Paper?**
   - Why is the question important?
   - What was unknown before this work?

3. **Background → Gap → Question**
   - Essential background only
   - Main hypothesis / scientific question

4. **Experimental / Analytical Framework**
   - One overview schematic
   - Key experiments, recordings, models, or analyses

5. **Key Findings**
   - Organize by claims, not figure numbers
   - For each result:  
     **Question → Evidence → Interpretation**

6. **Mechanism / Conceptual Advance**
   - What new mechanism or principle is proposed?
   - Correlation or causality?

7. **Critical Discussion**
   - Strongest evidence
   - Main limitation
   - Alternative explanation
   - Missing experiment

8. **Connection to Our Research**
   - What can we learn, reuse, or extend?

9. **Take-Home Messages**
   - 2–3 key messages
   - 2–3 discussion questions

## Slide Style

- **One slide, one message**
- Use **figures first**, text second
- Prefer **message-style titles**, not “Results” or “Methods”
- Crop and annotate figures instead of pasting full paper figures
- Keep text minimal: usually **≤3 bullets per slide**

## Suggested Length

**20–30 min / 15–22 slides**

> Focus on the scientific story, not every experimental detail.
